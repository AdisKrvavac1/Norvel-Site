HP Workforce Experience

HP Workforce Experience Platform (WXP) helps IT teams prevent device and software issues, manage their fleets and improve employee experience across every OS and app. The brief: one website that explains a complex enterprise platform and serves IT leaders, partners and buyers alike.

Behind the design

We designed the complete site as a system: a light, airy hero with isometric product illustrations, real dashboard UI to show the product rather than describe it, and a strong light/dark section rhythm that keeps long enterprise pages easy to scan.

  1. Structure: Map platform, offerings, partners and resources into a clear, scalable architecture.
  2. Show the product: Use real interface components and illustrations to make an abstract platform tangible.
  3. Systemise: Build page templates and components that cover every section of the site.

The result

A cohesive enterprise website across platform, offerings, partners and resources — clear for IT teams, credible for buyers and built to grow with the product.
